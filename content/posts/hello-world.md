Welcome to my new blog! This blog is fully integrated with **PagesCMS**, allowing me to easily publish new posts directly from a visual editor backed by GitHub.

### Why PagesCMS?
- **Git-backed**: All content lives in the repository as Markdown.
- **Easy UI**: Friendly rich-text editing experience.
- **No Database**: Static files load extremely fast and are highly secure.
